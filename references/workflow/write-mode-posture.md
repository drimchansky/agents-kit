# Write-Mode Posture: Delegation Is the Standing Posture

The one posture for the write-mode consumers registered in `./executor-routing.md` § *The registry and its authorization*. No consumer states a posture, a packet-cost prior, or a cadence of its own.

**Every unit delegates.** Each unit a registered consumer frames goes to an executor under the launch packet and gates `./executor-contract.md` fixes: one unit per launch, or several consecutive units in one launch where that file's § *Segment launches* and the consumer's binding sanction it. There is no size threshold, no last-unit branch, and no default to weigh. A consumer's binding may restrict which work is delegable at all (`fix-findings`'s Confirmed auto-path surface is the only such restriction today); inside that surface delegation is not a judgment call.

**Delegation owes a packet; the packet's cost is no reason to keep the unit.** Before launching, confirm every § *Launch packet* item is filled for this unit: its text and criterion, its edit surface, the context it cannot see for itself, the decision rule with prior decisions and boundaries, the domain guidance, the per-unit checks, the effective root and placement, the consumer label. Close a gap by reading, searching, or asking. A gap that can only be closed by running the unit is exception 1.

## The exceptions

Three, and only these. Each applies to one unit, not the run; the next unit delegates again unless its own facts reach one of the three. Only exception 2 can hold for a whole run, and only while its condition does.

1. **The unit cannot be specified before it runs.** Its shape is discovered by doing it: the next action depends on the last one's output, or mid-unit user interaction is expected. A unit that is merely small, large, or unfamiliar is specifiable and delegates.
2. **Delegation is unavailable in the environment.** The host cannot launch an executor: subagents unsupported, the adapter or its pinned model unavailable, or a live sandbox, permission setting, or standing session instruction forbidding it. Report what blocked it; never weaken or bypass a live security or permission boundary to delegate. A session instruction permitting agents only when the user asks blocks a model-invoked run, not a user-invoked one (`./executor-routing.md` § *The registry and its authorization*; `./skill-conventions.md` § *The invocation gate* tells which).
3. **The executor failed.** Unavailable, hung, host-failed, surface-escaping, or conflicting at merge. The consumer's **Fallback** in `./executor-contract.md` § *Bindings* takes the unit from there.

Every inline unit is **announced in chat as it happens and recorded in the consumer's report**, naming which exception applied. The record's shape is each consumer's own.

# Write-Mode Posture: Delegation Is the Standing Posture

The one posture for the write-mode consumers registered in `./executor-contract.md` § *Write-mode routing* — split out of that file, which keeps the consumer registry and the engine defaults. **This file is the single home for whether a unit delegates, and for the closed set of exceptions that keep one inline.** No consumer states a posture, a packet-cost prior, or a cadence of its own.

**Every unit delegates.** Each unit a registered consumer frames goes to an executor under the launch packet and gates `./executor-contract.md` fixes — one unit per launch, or several consecutive units in one launch where that file's § *Segment launches* and the consumer's binding sanction it — whatever the unit's size, wherever it sits in the run, and whether one unit remains or twelve. There is no size threshold, no last-unit branch, and no default to weigh: what a consumer's binding may restrict is *which* work is delegable at all — `fix-findings`'s Confirmed auto-path surface is the only such restriction today — and inside that surface delegation is not a judgment call. Judgment does not travel with the work: unit framing, each unit's outcome re-proof on the coordinator's own tree, the declared health boundary, the records, and every status stay with the coordinator per that file.

**A packet is what delegation owes, never a reason to keep the unit.** Before launching, confirm every § *Launch packet* item is filled for *this* unit — its text and its criterion, its edit surface, the context it depends on and cannot see for itself, the domain guidance, the effective root and the placement. A gap the coordinator can close by reading, searching, or asking is closed, and the cost of closing it decides nothing. A gap that can only be closed by running the unit is exception 1.

## The exceptions

Three, and only these. Each applies to **one unit**, not to the run: the next unit delegates again unless its own facts reach one of the three. Exception 2 is the only one that can hold for a whole run, and it holds exactly as long as its condition does.

1. **The unit cannot be specified before it runs.** Its shape is discovered by doing it — the next action depends on the last one's output, or mid-unit user interaction is expected. A unit that is merely small, large, or unfamiliar is not this one: those are specifiable, so they delegate.
2. **Delegation is unavailable in the environment.** The host cannot launch an executor — subagents unsupported, the adapter or its pinned model unavailable, or a live sandbox, permission setting, or standing session instruction forbidding it. Report what blocked it; never weaken or bypass a live security or permission boundary to delegate, and weigh a session instruction that permits agents *when the user asks* against the standing authorization in `./executor-contract.md` § *Write-mode routing* before treating it as a block.
3. **The executor failed.** Unavailable, hung, host-failed, surface-escaping, or conflicting at merge. The consumer's **Fallback** in `./executor-contract.md` § *Bindings* takes the unit from there, after whatever the § *Write-mode engine registry* degrade ladder leaves ahead of it.

Every inline unit is **announced in chat as it happens and recorded in the consumer's report**, naming which exception applied. That record is what keeps a standing posture from decaying silently into always-inline; the record's shape is each consumer's own.

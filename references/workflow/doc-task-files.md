# Doc-Task Files

The role files a documentation task (`**Domain:** documentation`) grows beyond the four core artifacts. Folder placement stays in `./task-layout.md`. The deliverable is the content's home, `result.md` stays the home of history, and a claim about an external copy's state appears only dated (`./one-home.md`).

- **Deliverable**: the work product, role-named for its kind (`adr.md`, `rfc.md`, or another clearly named doc). It carries its own `**Status:**` header, the document's lifecycle, separate from the plan's task lifecycle. The plan may point at it with an optional `**Deliverable:** [./adr.md](./adr.md)` header, but resolution never depends on that header: the deliverable is the folder's `.md` outside the six fixed-name role files (`CONTEXT.md`, `goals.md`, `plan.md`, `result.md`, `ticket.md`, `observations.md`) whose own header block, directly under the `#` title and above the first `##`, carries a `**Status:**` line. A status line inside fenced or quoted content does not count, and no other role below has such a header. Two candidates in one folder is a layout error to surface, not to guess between. Format bars and status values: `../documentation/adr-format.md`, `../documentation/rfc-format.md`.
- **Published pointer**: when a copy lives outside the folder, the deliverable's header says which copy is live truth with a dated `**Published:**` line, the URL plus the authority claim (`**Published:** <url> — page is live truth (applied 2026-07-28)`, or `… — local ahead of the page since 2026-07-28`). Exactly one side is authoritative at a time; the user flips or re-dates the line when the copies diverge or re-sync.
- **Outbound drafts**: paste-ready content the user sends or applies (`outreach.md`, `jira-drafts.md`, …). A draft that has gone out carries one dated free-text line beside it saying what was sent and when; an untouched draft carries none. `result.md` cites the file rather than restating it.
- **Research dossiers** (`research*.md`): sources read and facts extracted, each with its link. The deliverable cites dossier sections rather than restating them.
- **Review-evidence files** (`evidence-*.md`): verification artifacts backing a claim or review round, cited from the deliverable or result section that makes the claim.
- **Patch-instruction docs** (`<target>-update-*.md`): what to change, where, and the exact replacement text for a doc the agent does not own. Applied by the user, and carrying the same dated line as outbound drafts.

# Coordinator-Side Parallel Batch

Intake: `./executor-contract.md` § *Write-mode routing*. The open-leg rule below applies to every delegated unit, regardless of placement.

## Coordinator-side parallel batch

**Eligibility.** Require no direct/transitive dependency, pairwise disjoint declared edit surfaces, and verification executable in isolated copies. Same-artifact edits remain sequential (`CORE_RULES.md`). Missing or `none` surfaces run serially; infer no surface. Default to serial when disjointness is uncertain. Apply consumer-specific bounds, including implement-task's checkpoint and Touches requirements.

**Run.** Launch one native executor per eligible unit with a self-contained `./executor-contract.md` packet (`./executor-routing.md` § *Write-mode engine registry*). Each uses its own coordinator-created worktree, seeded from the shared tree including staged, unstaged, and untracked work. Executors cannot create, switch, or seed worktrees. Before any worktree diverges, capture one **batch baseline** manifest from the shared tree. Use that same manifest for every unit's check/apply.

Redirect each unit's writable caches, daemons, and workspace metadata into its worktree using tool-supported flags or variables. Register these as root-relative `--prune` paths. Automatic exclusions are `.git`, `node_modules`, and ignored untracked paths outside all surfaces (`../scripts/worktree-merge.md`). Inside a surface, ignored tool state still requires an explicit prune to avoid apply refusal. Prune only untracked paths; redirect tools away from tracked caches or generated bundles.

Keep unit output outside ignored paths: excluded output cannot be incorporated. Divergent ignored output inside a surface reports `NOT MEASURED`, increments `ignored-divergent`, and refuses apply. Name applicable non-checkout tool state and output that is neither tracked nor ignored.

The **change set** includes all content/presence differences from the baseline, tracked or untracked, excluding ignored content. Freeze the shared tree until every executor returns or its worktree is discarded. The coordinator runs no unit or serial fallback during that interval; wait under `./delegated-waiting.md` § *How to wait*.

**The verify criterion is retargeted, not rewritten.** Retarget shared-tree paths and explicit `cd` commands into the worktree. Carry both the verbatim criterion and executable retargeted form in the packet. Move only the root; preserve assertions and passing conditions. Gate 3 runs the original criterion on the integrated tree.

**An unexecutable leg is carried open, at any placement.** Identify unavailable browser/device/human checks before launch and mark them explicitly open in the packet. The executor proves executable legs and reports open ones unchanged. Keep the whole criterion; removing an open leg cannot make the unit pass. It remains unproved until that leg is verified in-session or through the user's best-available proxy. The consumer owns the resulting pause (`./implement-task-edges.md` § *An open criterion leg*).

**Merge in consumer order**: plan, frame, or severity order under `./executor-contract.md` § *Bindings*. Wait for every return or discard first. Unavailable/hung units enter serial fallback without a raw worktree delta.

Run gates with `node <kit-root>/scripts/worktree-merge.ts`; resolve the root through `./task-store.md` § *Resolving `<kit-root>`*. Read CLI and exit contracts in `../scripts/worktree-merge.md` before invocation. Without a kit root, perform these same gates manually. Manifests contain hashes; the coordinator separately captures restoration bytes.

1. **Returned-worktree surface check (`check`).** Compare all content/presence changes, including additions and deletions, against the batch baseline. An outside-surface change discards that worktree and enters serial fallback.
2. **Capture, incorporate, or fall back.** At the unit's ordered position, capture the exact shared state with a pre-unit manifest and byte copy. Apply a surface-checked worktree's modifications, additions, and deletions atomically through `apply`, using the **batch baseline**, not that pre-unit capture. Exclude worktree commits and branch merges. On conflict or failed incorporation verification, restore the exact pre-unit state using the landed/did-not-land report, discard, and fall back. Do not hand-resolve conflicts inside executor worktrees. Unavailable, hung, escaping, ignored-divergent, conflicting, and unverified units rerun only themselves serially on the current integrated tree. A unit returning a pending impactful choice (`./executor-contract.md` § *Write-mode routing*) is neither incorporated nor fallen back: discard its worktree without taking its edits, hold the unit, and ask; after the decision, relaunch the whole unit serially on the integrated tree with the decision in its packet. Check that rerun's surface against its immediate pre-unit capture (`./executor-contract.md` § *Write-mode routing*). If it escapes, restore the capture, discard its work, and stop under the consumer's Blocked binding (`./execution-bindings.md`); independent fix-findings units instead bucket the finding and continue. An escaping serial fallback receives no further retry.
3. **Re-prove the unit outcome.** After incorporation, run the complete integrated outcome tier: criterion plus domain per-unit checks (`./execution-loop.md` § *Two verification tiers*). This excludes health and cannot be replaced by the worktree report. Serial fallback reports take intake instead. On failure, remove all remaining batch worktrees before consumer Stop-the-Line/recovery; leave the failed outcome unrecorded. Cleanup alone does not fail the remaining units: independent fix-findings units enter serial fallback; others remain unstarted under Blocked. Account for each one.
4. **Expose and record.** After integrated proof passes, expose the incorporated delta from exact pre-unit state to resulting state, with consumer order and dependencies. Raw worktree deltas are merge inputs; incorporated sets are recovery evidence, not Git state. Record under the consumer binding (`./execution-bindings.md`).

Once all units incorporate, finish fallback, or are held on a pending choice, remove every executor worktree: verified merges use `remove`; discarded/failed/hung/held worktrees use `discard`. Only then run the consumer's single health boundary on the accumulated tree; a held unit's later relaunch is a serial unit under the consumer's own cadence. Individual merges owe no health pass. Executor worktrees are transient, without branches or commits; merge by diff-apply and remove them. Seed and merge against the run's task worktree where one exists, otherwise its checkout (`./task-delivery-edges.md` § *The task worktree is the run's shared tree*).

The consumer sets merge placement and health cadence. Implement-task checkpoint batches share that checkpoint's health pass.

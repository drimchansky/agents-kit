# Task Authorship: The Write-Surface Registry

## Files

- **`ticket.md`**, optional: upstream ask (`./ticket-format.md`), without Status. The user or `prepare-ticket` authors it; the user edits freely. `refine-idea` and `plan-task` derive context/goals from it. Reconcilers may make judged edits under `./reconciliation.md` § *The upstream ask is writable, and never rewritten quietly*. `implement-task` may correct execution-disproved claims under those same terms and its Correcting Grounding rules.
- **`CONTEXT.md`**: static grounding, capitalized, without Status. Created by `refine-idea`, `plan-task`, or `decompose-task`. Later edits are limited to `./task-lifecycle.md` § *Status values*' reconciliation carve-out and implement-task's execution-disproved corrections. Reconcilers annotate References/Open Questions or judge prose changes under `./reconciliation.md` § *Grounding docs change on evidence, never silently*.
- **`goals.md`**: acceptance, without Status. `plan-task` drafts it before the plan; users may author or edit freely. Reconcilers add, reword, or retire goals as judged edits with durable IDs under reconciliation's grounding rule. `implement-task` may correct goals, but never grades against its own semantic goal edit; the next run gates it. The task loader may repair proven identifier spelling and its references without changing acceptance meaning (`./task-layout.md` § *Reading a resolved folder*). Apart from that repair, no other skill authors goal content. `maintain` may rename legacy goal files but edits no content.
- **`plan.md`**: scope, steps, Verify criteria, and the task's lifecycle Status. `plan-task` creates it as to-do; `implement-task` transitions it. Both docs-to-reality composites and `reconcile-task` may repair downward or advance only on current-run verification (`./reconciliation.md` § *Strengthen only on verified evidence*). The task loader may update proven goal identifiers in scope and citations without altering steps or status.
- **`observations.md`**, optional: dated external-reference state, with Swept rather than Status. Format: `./task-observations.md`; scope: `./reconciliation-sweep.md` § *Scope*. Only reconcilers' sweeps rewrite it wholesale (§ *Ledger* there); no appends. `resume-task` reads dated lines without fetching. Absence is not drift.
- **`result.md`**: Current state above append-only history, without a Status header. `implement-task` creates it and appends execution, Blocked, In review, Acceptance, and Completed as owed by `./task-lifecycle.md` § *Companion result file*. Reconcilers append Reconciliation sections and may advance on current-run evidence. Docs-to-reality composites may also create missing result and remove Completed alongside a flip out of done.

The structural loader may correct proven identifier spellings and their references only in existing `CONTEXT.md`, `goals.md`, `plan.md`, and `result.md`, including append-only history. A reference in any other task Markdown, such as `ticket.md` or a deliverable, blocks a fresh-ID remap (`../scripts/task-state.md`). This narrow repair changes no acceptance wording, evidence, or lifecycle state (`./task-layout.md` § *Reading a resolved folder*).

The result's **`## Current state`** sits directly under the link header. `implement-task` and all reconcilers rewrite it in place as derived metadata, at most **1 KB**, carrying `_Updated: YYYY-MM-DD_`, a one-line status gloss, `**Pointers:**`, and `**Next:**`.

Pointers hold delivery identifiers such as branch, PR, SHA, and ticket (`./one-home.md` § *One home per fact*). Every writer carries an existing commit watermark forward unchanged unless reconciliation's watermark rule permits advancement (`./reconciliation-commits.md` § *The watermark*). `implement-task` creates or advances no watermark.

The digest is regenerable and never the sole home of a fact. Its gloss cannot claim a stronger state than plan Status. Refresh its narrative, Pointers, and Next from dated evidence even when the plan is done; a delivery fact can change after acceptance. A new PR merge updates the current account without reopening the plan or rewriting Acceptance (`./reconciliation.md` § *Current state refresh*). Live executing/blocked/in-review plans expect this block. Legacy results without a block are conformant; create it on the next write.

An optional **`## Decision log`** below Current state's closing separator is an append-only index. Add dated one-line pointers to the actual decision in result, context, plan, or store-level DECISIONS. When a decision predates result creation, link its original dated context or plan anchor here after creating result. Never copy decision text into the index. Everything below Current state is append-only, apart from the registered closing-Completed metadata change above.

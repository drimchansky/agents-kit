# Task Authorship: The Write-Surface Registry

## Files

- **`ticket.md`**, optional: upstream ask (`./ticket-format.md`), without Status. The user or `prepare-ticket` authors it; the user edits freely. `refine-idea`, `choose-approach`, and `plan-task` derive their permitted grounding from it. Task writers may correct the upstream ask under `./task-writes.md` § *Ticket and group corrections*. `implement-task` may correct execution-disproved claims under those same terms and its Correcting Grounding rules. `start-group` may make those judged edits in the tickets of every task below its group outside Archive and Backlog, including tasks in nested groups.
- **`CONTEXT.md`**: static grounding, capitalized, without Status. Created by `refine-idea`, `choose-approach`, `plan-task`, or `decompose-task`. `start-group` also scaffolds a missing one in a task below its group before a change-set edit there. Planning records its own settled context answers under `./task-writes.md`. Apply `./task-writes.md` to task grounding and reference edits. A group change set may correct any affected task below its group outside Archive and Backlog, including nested groups.
- **`approaches.md`**, optional: comparison and selection evidence under `./task-approaches.md`. Its Decision field is not lifecycle status. `choose-approach` alone authors and revises it; other workflows cite its original anchors.
- **`goals.md`**: acceptance, without Status. `plan-task` drafts it before the plan; users may author or edit freely. Writers correcting goals under `./task-writes.md` keep durable IDs. `implement-task` may correct goals, but never grades against its own semantic goal edit; the next run gates it. The task loader may repair proven identifier spelling and its references without changing acceptance meaning (`./task-layout.md` § *Reading a resolved folder*). `maintain` may rename legacy goal files but edits no content.
- **`plan.md`**: scope, steps, Verify criteria, and the task's lifecycle Status. `plan-task` creates it as to-do; `implement-task` transitions it. A task writer repairs or advances progress on current-run proof under `./task-progress.md`. The task loader may update proven goal identifiers in scope and citations without altering steps or status.
- **`observations.md`**, optional: dated external-reference state, with Swept rather than Status. Only an explicit source check reads and replaces the ledger under `./source-check.md` and `./task-observations.md`. Absence is not drift.
- **`result.md`**: current execution and acceptance records. Writers use [Task result](./task-result.md) and the [plan status requirements](./task-lifecycle.md#companion-result-file).

- **`history.md`**, optional: `start-group` and other task writers append the evidence for a settled task decision, including the wording of a changed ticket or grounding statement, under [Task history](./task-history.md).

The structural loader may correct proven identifier spellings and their references only in existing `CONTEXT.md`, `goals.md`, `plan.md`, and `result.md`. A reference in any other task Markdown, such as `approaches.md`, `ticket.md`, or a deliverable, blocks a fresh-ID remap (`../scripts/task-state.md`). This narrow repair changes no acceptance wording, evidence, or lifecycle state (`./task-layout.md` § *Reading a resolved folder*).
